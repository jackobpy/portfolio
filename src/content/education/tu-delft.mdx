## Computer Science and Engineering

I graduated **cum laude** with a GPA of **9.12/10**. My studies combined the foundations of computer science with practical software engineering, and I followed the systems track, focusing on how software interacts with operating systems, networks, security, and embedded hardware. I completed every course on my first attempt.

For my bachelor’s thesis, I worked on [representation learning for high-dimensional single-cell genomics](/projects/single-cell-representations) using variational autoencoders. I completed my 30-ECTS minor as an [exchange semester at ETH Zürich](/education/eth-exchange), where I focused on probabilistic artificial intelligence and network security.

Alongside my degree, I worked as a [teaching assistant at TU Delft](/experience/tu-delft-teaching) for Web and Database Technology, Information and Data Management, and Concepts of Programming Languages.
