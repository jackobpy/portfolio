## International Baccalaureate

I completed the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme (IB DP) at Staszic High School in Lublin, graduating with 44 out of 45 points.

The IB DP is a two-year secondary-school programme built around six academic subjects and a common core. Each subject is assessed through several components rather than a single final test: depending on the course, these can include multiple written examinations, oral assessments, laboratory work, essays, and independent research projects. Students also complete Theory of Knowledge (TOK), an Extended Essay (EE), and Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS).

Each of the six subjects is graded from 1 to 7, for a maximum of 42 subject points. TOK and the Extended Essay can add up to three core points, bringing the highest possible diploma score to 45.
