## Exchange studies

I was selected and nominated as one of two students in my year for an exchange at ETH Zürich through the Swiss European Mobility Programme. During the semester, I completed **30 credits** and received a SEMP Scholarship.

The exchange gave me the opportunity to study across several areas of computer science. Among my courses were:

- **Probabilistic Artificial Intelligence (PAI)**, covering decision-making and learning under uncertainty
- **Network Security (NS)**, focused on the principles and mechanisms behind secure networked systems
- **Concepts of Object-Oriented Programming (COOP)**, examining programming-language design and advanced object-oriented concepts
- **Hands-On Deep Learning**, a practical course built around implementing and experimenting with modern neural-network methods

PAI became especially important to me: after completing it during the exchange, I later returned to the course as a [teaching assistant](/experience/eth-teaching) during my master’s at ETH.
