I’m Jakub Fręchowicz, a computer scientist based in Zürich. I am currently studying for an MSc in Computer Science at [ETH Zürich](/education/eth-zurich), where I focus on autonomous vehicles and the systems that allow them to perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and act reliably.

Before moving to Zürich, I completed my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at [TU Delft](/education/tu-delft), cum laude. During my bachelor’s, I worked across machine learning, software engineering, and mathematics—from studying [representation learning for single-cell genomics](/projects/single-cell-representations) to developing an agentic AI tool with [Google Cloud’s Enablement Team](/experience/google-cloud).

## Teaching and explaining

Teaching has become an important part of my work. At ETH, I am a [teaching assistant for Probabilistic Artificial Intelligence](/experience/eth-teaching), a course I first took during my exchange. At TU Delft, I spent two years with [PRIME](/experience/prime), working with mathematics professors on educational software and Manim animations that make abstract linear algebra more visual.

These experiences taught me that building and explaining are closely connected. Whether I am designing software, working through a machine learning problem, or preparing a tutorial, I enjoy finding the structure in a difficult idea and turning it into something clear and useful.

## Where I’m heading

I am now moving deeper into autonomous systems, particularly the machine learning and software behind autonomous vehicles. I am interested in how these systems combine perception, reasoning under uncertainty, and reliable decision-making—and in the work required to make those parts function together outside a controlled setting.

This site brings together the projects, teaching, and writing that have shaped that direction.
