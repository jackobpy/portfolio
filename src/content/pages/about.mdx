I’m Jakub Fręchowicz, based in Zürich and currently studying for an MSc in Computer Science at [ETH Zürich](/education/eth-zurich). My interests extend across architecture, visualisations, software, and education—especially the ways complex systems can be structured and communicated clearly.

Before moving to Zürich, I completed my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at [TU Delft](/education/tu-delft), cum laude. During my bachelor’s, I worked across machine learning, software engineering, and mathematics—from studying [representation learning for single-cell genomics](/projects/single-cell-representations) to developing an agentic AI tool with [Google Cloud’s Enablement Team](/experience/google-cloud).

## Teaching and explaining

Teaching has become an important part of my work. At TU Delft, I taught across several courses and spent two years with [PRIME](/experience/prime), working with mathematics professors on educational software and Manim animations that make abstract linear algebra more visual.

These experiences taught me that building and explaining are closely connected. Whether I am designing software, working through a machine learning problem, or preparing a tutorial, I enjoy finding the structure in a difficult idea and turning it into something clear and useful.

## What interests me

I’m drawn to architecture in its broadest sense: how individual parts relate to a whole, whether in a building, a piece of software, or an explanation. Visualisation is a natural extension of that interest—the craft of making structure visible without flattening its complexity.

This site brings together the projects, teaching, and writing that have shaped that perspective.
