I’m Jakub Fręchowicz, a computer scientist based in Zürich. My interests sit at the intersection of software engineering, machine learning, and autonomous systems.

I completed my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at TU Delft, cum laude, and have been admitted to the MSc Computer Science programme at ETH Zürich.

## Building and explaining

My work includes a software development project with Google’s Enablement Team, representation learning for single-cell genomics, and educational animations for linear algebra.

Teaching has also been an important part of my experience: helping students through lab sessions, supporting courses, and making difficult ideas easier to understand.

## What interests me

I’m particularly interested in autonomous vehicles and the systems that connect perception, reasoning, and action. This website brings together my projects, education, and writing as that work develops.
