## Building software for the Enablement Team

I worked with [Micha Lionarons](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michalionarons/) from Google Cloud’s Enablement Team on an **Automated Sales Enablement Program Builder**: a web application that brings the creation and evaluation of sales enablement curricula into one tool.

The work was carried out under an internship agreement for **CSE2000: Software Project**, as part of my [BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at TU Delft](/education/tu-delft).

The tool combined curriculum creation, management, and evaluation with agentic AI features to support the enablement workflow. The aim was to help the team turn training content into structured programmes and assess those curricula within the same application.
