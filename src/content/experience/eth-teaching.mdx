## Probabilistic Artificial Intelligence

I was selected as a Teaching Assistant for **Probabilistic Artificial Intelligence (PAI)**, taught by [Andreas Krause](https://las.inf.ethz.ch/krausea) at ETH Zürich. I joined the tutorials team early in my master’s after completing the course during my exchange a year earlier with a grade of **5.75 / 6**.

PAI is widely regarded as one of the most demanding courses in the ETH Zürich computer science curriculum. Being invited to return as a teaching assistant was a meaningful opportunity to revisit the material from the other side of the classroom and help students work through it.

## Tutorials team

As part of the tutorials team, I give tutorial sessions, prepare homework sheets, answer questions about the lecture and tutorial material on Moodle, and hold Q&A sessions. The role combines careful preparation with the ability to explain difficult probabilistic and machine learning concepts clearly, adapting to what students need in the moment.
